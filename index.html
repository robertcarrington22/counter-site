What we fight

The bills people accept because arguing feels worse.

Hospital and doctor bills

Duplicate charges, wrong codes, facility fees that shouldn't be there, and the cash rate nobody told you about. About one in three medical bills contains an error.

Typical find: same CT scan billed twice

Contractor quotes

Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, remodels. We compare every line against real local pricing and get you two more bids while you sleep.

Typical find: "up to 10 sheets included" decking padding

Car repair estimates

Labor hours versus book time, parts markup, and the "while we're in there" add-ons at 48,000 miles. We also check your VIN for open recalls and warranty coverage first, which sometimes makes the whole bill disappear.

Typical find: 4.5 hours billed on a 2-hour job

Coming next: rent renewals and insurance total-loss offers. We don't handle insurance claims, legal disputes, or anything that needs a license. We'll tell you when you need a professional, and who to call.

How we know

The verdict is a comparison, not an opinion.

Every line on your bill gets checked against a public or regional benchmark. We tell you how confident we are, and we say "fair" when it's fair.

Medical bills

Each code is compared to the Medicare rate and the hospital's own published price list. We look for duplicates, upcoded visit levels, out-of-network charges on in-network visits, and providers you never saw.

Contractor quotes

Line items are compared to regional cost guides and every quote we've reviewed in your area. We flag missing permits, vague allowances, and deposits above your state's legal cap.

Car repair

Labor is checked against published book time for your exact vehicle. Parts are checked against retail. Add-on services are checked against the manufacturer's maintenance schedule.

Questions

The ones people ask before they send the photo.

Is this a person or an AI?
Both. AI reads the bill, checks every line, and drafts the dispute. A person reviews every verdict and every counter before it goes out, and during our launch a person makes the calls. Whenever an AI assistant is on a call, we say so in the first sentence, and we say the call is recorded.
Will you really call my hospital or my contractor?
Yes, once you tell us to. Most medical disputes are actually won in writing, so we start with the itemized-bill request and the written dispute, then call for status and the self-pay rate. You see the plan first, you approve it, and you get the notes or transcript after. You can stop us at any point with one text.
What if I already paid?
Send it anyway. Refunds on billing errors and double payments are common, and you'll know what to look for next time.
How do you know what a fair price is?
For medical bills we compare against Medicare rates and the hospital's own published prices. For contractors and repair shops we use regional cost guides and every quote we've reviewed in your area. We tell you how confident we are, and when we aren't.
What do you do with my bill?
Read it, fight it if you ask, and keep it in your file so the next one is faster. We never sell it or share it with anyone but the party we're negotiating with. Delete everything with one text.
Are you lawyers? Insurance adjusters?
No. If your situation needs one, we'll say so and point you to someone good. Counter is not a law firm, a public adjuster, or a medical provider.
What counts as "savings" for the 20% option?
The difference between the patient-responsibility amount on your current statement and what you actually pay after our work. We state that number in writing before you choose the percentage option. Reductions from financial assistance or charity care programs are excluded from the percentage entirely, and the percentage is only offered on medical bills.
Why would I pay $29 when the verdict is free?
The free verdict tells you whether the bill is padded, by roughly how much, and what kind of problem we found. The kit gives you the specific lines and amounts, the letters already written with your account details, and the script for the call. It's the difference between knowing you're overpaying and having the thing you send to fix it.
My car is at the shop right now. Is there time for this?
Yes. Text the shop "please don't start any work yet, I'll get back to you within a couple of hours," then send us the estimate. The verdict takes about ten minutes, and the kit includes us calling two independent shops for comparison quotes while you wait. If the repair is a safety issue, we'll say so and help you get it done today at a fair price rather than tell you to shop around.
What if the verdict says it's fair?
Then it's fair, and we'll tell you the two or three things worth asking before you sign. We don't manufacture fights.
